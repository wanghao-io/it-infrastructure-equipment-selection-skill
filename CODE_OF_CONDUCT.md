# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We welcome contributors regardless of experience, identity, background, language, vendor preference, or employer. Participate with technical honesty, patience, and respect.

Expected behavior includes:

- critique claims, evidence, and code rather than people;
- distinguish verified facts from assumptions and estimates;
- disclose relevant vendor or commercial interests;
- protect confidential infrastructure and procurement information;
- accept correction and make room for less-experienced contributors;
- keep disagreement focused on project requirements and reproducible evidence.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, deliberate disclosure of private information, impersonation, spam, vendor astroturfing, or sustained disruption of project work.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, Issues, Discussions, or other contributions that violate this policy. Proportionate responses may include a private warning, temporary participation limits, or a permanent ban.

Report public platform abuse with GitHub's reporting tools. For a private project report, use the repository's [private reporting form](https://github.com/wanghao-io/it-infrastructure-equipment-selection-skill/security/advisories/new) and prefix the title with `[Conduct]`.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. A maintainer named in a report must not be the sole decision-maker; when no independent maintainer is available, the project will use GitHub Trust & Safety or a mutually acceptable external reviewer.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone represents the project in public community interactions.
